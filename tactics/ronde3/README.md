## How it was classified

1. Every video of every channel was listed with yt-dlp (`--flat-playlist`, videos + streams tab, plus
   playlist membership; for channels with more than 30 playlists only the playlists whose title mentions
   Old World/tactics/guide/rules). One channel at a time, `--sleep-requests 1` plus pauses.
2. The round-1 rules from `tactics/selectie.mjs` (copied, not imported, because that script writes
   `selectie.json` when loaded) gave a first soort per video. Additions: Renegade Crowns as 18th army,
   shorts under 3 minutes, and on mixed channels a title or playlist must name Old World.
3. Every title was then read by hand per channel. The rules over-select (e.g. "Best Chariots tier list"
   or "Character Creation" for the roleplaying game hit the tactics words), so **only videos confirmed by
   hand count as tactics**; rule hits not confirmed were rejected. Same line as round 1: general technique,
   unit roles, matchups and specific tricks are tactics, even when tied to one army; faction guides
   ("How to win with X", "How to play X", unit rankings) are army-review.
4. For unclear titles the description was read (one request per video). 14 were decided that way; 12
   stay unclear, mostly lore deep dives (spell review or how to use the lore?) and two magic overviews of
   Mountain Miniatures without a useful description.
5. Duplicates of `public/tactics/bronnen.json` and `tactics/selectie.json` are excluded (none of the
   kept videos was already listed). Upload date and length come from a per-video metadata request.
6. `leger` is set when the advice is tied to one army (for "how to beat X" videos: the army being beaten,
   unless the video is clearly from one army's side, e.g. Dwarfs vs Empire).
